Questions I get asked

Before you call
How is a Quality of Earnings report different from an audit?

An audit asks whether the financial statements comply with the accounting standards. A QoE asks a narrower and more practical question: how much of the reported earnings are real, repeatable, and likely to still be there after closing. It is forward-looking and buyer-focused, and it is not an assurance engagement.

How long does it take?

Most engagements run two to four weeks from the day the document request is filled. The clock is usually set by how quickly the target produces records, not by the analysis. Compressed timelines are possible and priced accordingly.

Can you act as both my analyst and my paralegal?

Sometimes, and it has to be assessed file by file. The two roles carry different duties, and paralegal representation is limited to the scope permitted in Ontario. Where a matter falls outside that scope or a conflict exists, I will tell you at the scoping call and refer you to a lawyer.

What drives the fee?

Deal size, how many entities and years are in scope, the state of the books, and turnaround. Clean records on a single entity sit at the bottom of the range. A multi-entity group with reconstructed statements sits at the top. You get a fixed quote after the scoping call, before any work starts.

Do you work outside Ontario?

Financial analysis, underwriting, and consulting work travels anywhere and is handled remotely. Paralegal representation is limited to Ontario, where I am licensed.
